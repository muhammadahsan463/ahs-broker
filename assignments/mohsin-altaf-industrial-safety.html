1. Safety

Safety means to stay free from danger, injury and loss. It keeps the worker and the machines safe from any accident in the workshop.

2. Safety Rules of Industry

  1. Always wear a helmet, gloves, goggles and safety shoes.
  2. Do not run a machine without training.
  3. Switch off the power before any repair.
  4. Keep the floor clean and dry.
  5. Do not block the passages.
  6. Tell the supervisor if you see any danger.

3. Causes of Accident

  1. Carelessness of the worker.
  2. Working without safety equipment.
  3. No proper training.
  4. Faulty machines and broken tools.
  5. Poor light and a slippery floor.
  6. Not following the safety rules.

4. Safe Acts

  1. Wearing a helmet, gloves and safety shoes.
  2. Using the right tool for the work.
  3. Following the safety rules.
  4. Keeping the workplace clean.
  5. Switching off the machine after the work.
  6. Telling the supervisor about any danger.

5. Unsafe Acts

  1. Working without a helmet or gloves.
  2. Running a machine without training.
  3. Removing the guard from a machine.
  4. Using a broken tool.
  5. Running or playing in the workshop.
  6. Ignoring the safety rules.
