1. Define Safety

Safety is the condition in which a person is protected from danger, injury, and loss. In a workshop, laboratory, or industry, safety means keeping workers, equipment, and materials free from harm. It depends on safe methods of work, correct use of tools, and control of hazards before they cause damage. Safety is a duty of every worker and of the management that runs the workplace.

2. Safety Rules of Industry

Safety rules of industry are the instructions followed to prevent accidents. Every worker should wear the required protective equipment, such as a helmet, gloves, goggles, and safety shoes. Machines must have guards, and the power supply must be switched off before repair. The workplace should be kept clean, and passages must stay clear. Unsafe conditions should be reported to the supervisor at once.

3. Causes of Accident

Accidents are caused by unsafe acts, unsafe conditions, or both together. Unsafe acts include ignoring rules, working without training, and not using protective equipment. Unsafe conditions include faulty machines, poor lighting, slippery floors, and exposed wires. Fatigue, hurry, and weak supervision also raise the chance of an accident. Most accidents can be prevented when these causes are removed and safety rules are followed.
